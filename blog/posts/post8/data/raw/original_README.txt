These are replication materials for "Cash Transfers as a Response to COVID-19: Experimental Evidence from Kenya" by Wyatt Brooks, Kevin Donovan, Terence r. Johnson, and Jackline Oluouch-Aridi.

Description of files:

1. WHO-COVID-19-global-data.csv : This is COIVD-19 case numbers by country from the World Health Organization. It is used to measure cases in Kenya during the study period. 

2. WHOKenya.do : creates graph that puts our study timeline onto WHO COVID-19 numbers for Kenya

3. BDJO_Dataset.dta : this is the main RCT data. It is the complete, raw data. All adjustments are made in AnalyzeDataset.do.

4. AnalyzeDataset.do : this is the main file that creates all the results for the paper (net of the single graph produced in WHOKenya.do). 

5. BDJO_COVID_Instrument.xls : survey instrument

For all .do files, tables are placed in */tex_tables/ and figures are placed in */computed_graphs/ . They are labeled by their number in the paper.
